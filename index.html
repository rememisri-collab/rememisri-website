Brand Story

Reme · Remembrance

Reme derives from Remember, standing for remembrance and collection. It preserves every precious moment and irreplaceable memory, locking the beauty of time forever.

Reme 取自英文 Remember，寓意铭记与珍藏，定格生命里所有温柔瞬间，封存时光美好。

Misri · Middle Eastern Soul

Misri originates from Arabic مصري, meaning Egyptian & Ancient Egyptian style, carrying the mysterious elegance of the Nile and royal light luxury texture.

Misri 源自阿拉伯语，代表古埃及中东风情；谐音 Miss，寄托心底思念与牵挂，赋予品牌双重浪漫内核。
